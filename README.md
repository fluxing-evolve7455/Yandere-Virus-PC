## 🕹️ About

Yandere Virus is a PC action-indie horror game developed by Moka Sphere and SCON and published by Moka Sphere. After a fun trip turns into a nightmare, players awaken in a mysterious graveyard where a precious friend has been infected by the Yandere Virus. Explore the area alone or with up to three friends, gather materials and Platonic Pumpkins, craft a cure, rescue the infected friend, and escape while avoiding relentless pursuit and mutated plants. Proximity voice chat, cooperative tension, and unpredictable threats give every expedition a chaotic survival-horror edge.

Yandere Virus is for players who enjoy comedic psychological horror, tense survival challenges, stealthy escapes, and chaotic cooperative gameplay.

## ✨ Features

- 🏆 **Achievement Challenges** — Earn achievements by surviving the virus outbreak and completing challenging objectives.
- 👥 **Four-Player Co-op** — Explore and survive alone or cooperate online with up to three friends.
- 🧪 **Cure Crafting** — Collect scattered materials and combine them into a cure for the infected friend.
- 🏃 **Relentless Pursuit** — Stay ahead of the infected friend, whose distorted behavior turns every chase into a panic-filled escape.
- 🪦 **Graveyard Setting** — Investigate a mysterious and hostile environment filled with hidden resources and sudden threats.
- 🎃 **Platonic Pumpkin Hunts** — Search the graveyard for rare Platonic Pumpkins needed to create the cure.
- 🌿 **Mutated Plants** — Watch for hostile plants that can attack without warning throughout the graveyard.
- 🎙️ **Proximity Voice Chat** — Coordinate nearby with teammates while distance and danger shape every conversation.
